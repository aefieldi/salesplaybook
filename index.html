Why you ask.

No one is raising their hand to hand you thousands of dollars. It's just not happening. You have to ask.

✦

Some people have a soul contract with you.

They need to work with you, and they are going to struggle until you show up and sell them something. The offer is how they find you.

✦

You didn't come here to serve everybody.

You came here for a very specific purpose: to serve your people. You are doing your people a disservice if you don't ask for the sale.

✦

Abundance aligns itself with the consciousness that can contain it.

That's the number one spiritual law of money. You are the vessel. You get to be spiritual and wealthy. If you don't believe that, the ask never leaves your mouth.

So at the end of every paid reading or audit, you say one sentence: "I know we covered a lot today. Did you want to integrate and figure all of this out by yourself, or would you like to hear what it would be like for us to work together?" Everything on this page is there to get you to that sentence.

If you remember nothing else

The three laws.

Everything else on this page is detail. These three are the game.

i

Sending links after the call is where sales go to die.

We never do it. Not the payment link, not the "info," not the "let me send you the details." Money moves while you're both still on the call. Once they hang up, the momentum is gone and life takes over. The only link you ever send is a payment link to someone who just told you they're ready to join right now.

ii

Two moments decide the call: the frame and the ask.

The first two minutes set who's leading. The last two minutes are where you ask for the business. Everything in between just feeds those two.

iii

Uncover obstacles before they become objections.

Money, time, a partner who "needs to weigh in." If it comes up at the end, it's an objection. If you surface it in the middle, it's just information and you can work with it.

Before the call

Your application asks three things.

If your booking form doesn't hit these, fix it today. You walk in already knowing the story, and you use their own words back to them.

NowWhere are you right now?Revenue, clients, members, what their week actually looks like.
NextWhere do you want to be?In 90 days, 6 months, a year. The more specific, the easier the ask.
The gapWhat's in the way? What have you already tried?Every course, coach and hack. That's the real pain.

On the call, don't ask why it didn't work. Ask "what was good about that?" They'll tell you what was good and what was bad, and you never have to make it a negative.

When they don't say yes (yet)

  
What they say. What you say.

  
Yes is great. No is great. Indecision is the only thing you can't work with. Your whole job here is to turn a maybe into a clean answer.

  







  
Readings, audits & strategy calls

  
Give the big picture. Go deep on one.

  
On a reading you'll talk more, and that's fine. But you're still not solving every problem on the call. You're showing them the map.

  
    
    
  • "Here's where I see us going. This is what I'd suggest: one, two, three."Three big-picture moves. Not twelve.
    • 
    
  • "Which one of these would you like to dive deeper into today?"They choose. Go deep on that one and take notes the whole time.
    • 
    
  • "I know we covered a lot today. Tell me, was this helpful for you?"Let them answer. Then you're at the top of the coaster. Make the ask.
    • 
  

  

    
The one thing you do send afterA recap of what you covered, for calls they've already paid for. Run the transcript through Claude so they can actually remember the reading. That's delivery, not selling.

    
Yes on the call? Their reading countsIf they decide on the call to join your ongoing container, what they paid for the reading or audit goes toward it. If they decide later, that's fine too, and they pay the full investment. Deciding in the room is what earns the credit.

    
What you don't send afterA payment link, a sales page, a "here's how it works" email, or a PDF of deliverables. If you're about to hit send on any of those, they should have paid on the call.

  







  
Sell in your design

  
Your chart tells you how. Never whether.

  
Nobody's design says "don't make the ask." Here's how each type makes it without abandoning themselves.

  

    
ProjectorThe booked call is the invitation. You've been invited to guide, and guiding includes telling them what it costs to go further with you.

    
GeneratorThey booked, you're responding. Let your energy show when it's a fit, then say it out loud. Your gut lighting up isn't the ask. The ask is the ask.

    
Manifesting GeneratorYou'll want to skip steps. Skip the deliverables list. Never skip the pain, the vision or the permission.

    
ManifestorYou're here to inform. Tell them what you see, what you'd do and what it costs, clearly. Don't wait for them to ask about working together.

    
ReflectorYou mirror the room. Script the call so their doubt doesn't turn into yours. You can take time on your own big decisions. Theirs get made on the call.

    
Open solar plexusYou feel their discomfort at the price like it's yours. It isn't. Script it, stay neutral, hold the silence. Sales is service.

  







  
The don't list

  
We never do this.

  

    
✕
Send links or emails after the call.Payment happens on the call. Onboarding gets booked on the call.

    
✕
List deliverables nobody asked for.How many calls, how often, which Voxer hours. They're paying for the transformation. If they ask, answer in one line and ask a question back.

    
✕
Pitch without permission.Always ask if they want to hear what working together looks like. Always.

    
✕
Offer a payment plan before they ask.Say the full price. If it's too much, they tell you the number.

    
✕
Solve every problem on the call.Your job is to uncover the pain, not to fix it for free in 45 minutes.

    
✕
Pull someone in.If you have to pull them in, you'll have to pull them along. That's no fun for either of you.
